As the world rapidly digitizes, senior citizens and grandparents are increasingly left struggling to keep pace — booking appointments, managing medications, paying bills, staying socially connected with family, or even identifying scams often become sources of stress and dependency rather than convenience. Most digital tools today are designed for tech-savvy, younger users, leaving seniors feeling excluded, overwhelmed, or vulnerable in an increasingly online-first world.
The challenge is to design and build a GenAI-powered website that serves as an intelligent, accessible, and trustworthy daily companion for senior citizens — helping them navigate everyday tasks with ease, confidence, and independence. The solution should go beyond a simple AI chatbot, using Generative AI to simplify complex information, anticipate needs, offer proactive assistance, and create a genuinely supportive digital experience tailored to the pace, comfort, and understanding of older users.
Participants are encouraged to explore a wide range of possibilities not just limited to AI-driven conversational assistance.
Ultimately, the goal is to build a solution that makes technology feel less like a barrier and more like a caring, capable companion — one that a senior citizen could use confidently, even without anyone else's help.)
